About Codebreakers Consultancy

Strategic clarity for complex data landscapes.

We help organisations move from fragmented data, ambitious technology plans and uncertainty to clear, practical action.

Codebreakers Consultancy helps organisations unlock the value hidden within their data.

Businesses are under increasing pressure to use Data, AI and Machine Learning to stay competitive, but many are held back by fragmented legacy and cloud systems, inconsistent data, unclear ownership and no clear roadmap for change.

We provide the strategy, architecture, governance and delivery leadership needed to turn that complexity into a clear path forward.

We work with leadership teams to understand the business challenge, assess the existing data and technology landscape, identify where the greatest opportunities exist and create a practical strategy for delivering them.

From data strategy and target architecture through to transformation roadmaps, governance, programme leadership, technical assurance and vendor advisory, Codebreakers Consultancy provides the expertise needed to take an organisation from ambition to execution.

We do not sell technology for technology's sake. We help businesses understand where data, analytics, automation and AI can genuinely create value, then provide the structure, leadership and technical direction required to make it happen.

Mine the data. Find the gold.

Experience across: media and broadcasting, financial operations, logistics and transport, supply chain, restaurants and hospitality, regulated organisations, travel and tourism, e-commerce, payments, digital and mobile technologies.
